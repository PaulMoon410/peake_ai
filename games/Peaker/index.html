How to Deposit PEK from MetaMask/EVM:
  • Send your PEK (or swap/wrapped PEK) from your EVM wallet to the swap.crypto bridge contract on Hive Engine's EVM chain.
  • In the transaction memo, enter your Hive username (e.g., peakecoin.matic).
  • After the bridge processes your deposit, your PEK will appear on Hive Engine and be available to play.
  • Note: Withdrawals and rewards are sent from peakecoin.bnb on Hive Engine. If a payout fails, it will be queued and reviewed.
All gameplay is provably fair and on-chain. For help, see our Discord support channel below.
